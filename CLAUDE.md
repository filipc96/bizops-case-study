Recaps go in brain/knowledge/meetings/YYYY-MM-DD-brand-slug-meeting-name.md (`RESOLVER.md:28`, plus existing pages' suffix) on the template: front matter with `source: inbox/<file>`, Summary of 3–6 bullets, Action items, Open questions, Flags for a person (`RESOLVER.md:41-62`).

A line that fails the 3-question check stays out; if it matters, note under Flags that something was held back, without repeating it (`SANITIZER.md:21-26`).
