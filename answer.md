## The flow, end to end
1. **Find:** Tomas Leal, creator specialist ([Marcus's page](brain/knowledge/people/marcus-obi.md)), sources food or baking creators with 5K–100K followers ([12 Aug](brain/knowledge/meetings/2026-08-12-kettle-and-crumb-affiliates-kickoff.md)).
2. **Approve:** Dana owns affiliates and creator approvals ([her page](brain/knowledge/people/dana-reyes.md)), and approves each creator within 48 hours of getting the list ([3 Sep](brain/knowledge/meetings/2026-09-03-kettle-and-crumb-affiliates-deep-dive.md)).
3. **Plans:** 15% commission on the open plan for all creators, 20% on the targeted plan for the top 10 ([12 Aug](brain/knowledge/meetings/2026-08-12-kettle-and-crumb-affiliates-kickoff.md)).
4. **Sample:** one box per approved creator, sent only after they accept the targeted plan ([3 Sep](brain/knowledge/meetings/2026-09-03-kettle-and-crumb-affiliates-deep-dive.md)). Samples now go through Jess ([24 Sep](brain/knowledge/meetings/2026-09-24-kettle-and-crumb-monthly-business-review.md)).
5. **Post and pay:** creators post, and commission is paid through TikTok Shop ([12 Aug](brain/knowledge/meetings/2026-08-12-kettle-and-crumb-affiliates-kickoff.md)).
6. **Report:** a Wednesday summary of new affiliates, posts and affiliate GMV ([3 Sep](brain/knowledge/meetings/2026-09-03-kettle-and-crumb-affiliates-deep-dive.md)). From 23 Sep it also shows which creators drove the most sales ([22 Sep](brain/knowledge/meetings/2026-09-22-kettle-and-crumb-weekly.md)).
